## Short description

Read branch and wind cues, choose a lane, and lock where one jacaranda blossom
will land in this six-round prediction game.

## Long description

One Lucky Bloom is a small, original prediction game about reading a stylised
jacaranda canopy. Each bloom begins in one of five lanes. Watch the upper
branch, wind, and lower twig cues, choose the landing lane, then lock the guess.
Earlier correct locks earn a larger timing bonus.

A run has six fixed, deterministic rounds and takes about 42 seconds after the
brief first-play explanation. Results stay on the device as a personal best;
there are no accounts or online leaderboards. The local exam-season luck story
is presented as a fable, not study advice. Enjoy real trees without picking or
disturbing blossoms.

## Controls

- **Touch / pointer:** tap a numbered lane, then tap **Lock lane**.
- **Keyboard:** press `1`–`5`, Left/Right Arrow, Home, or End to choose; Tab to
  **Lock lane**, then press Space or Enter.
- **Settings:** Sound is off by default. Reduced motion can be toggled at any
  time. **Reset local data** clears only this game's settings and personal best.

## Accessibility and motion

The game uses native buttons, a labelled radio group, visible focus, live status
text, and a textual result ledger. It supports keyboard-only play and 48 px lane
targets. System reduced-motion preference is honoured on first load, and the
in-game reduced-motion toggle replaces continuous movement with stepped changes.
Game information is not conveyed by colour alone. This is not a claim of formal
accessibility certification.

## Content and privacy statement

in-app purchases, chat, accounts, user IDs, or location collection. It stores
only settings, completed-run count, and the best score/catch count in local
browser storage. Play remains available when storage is missing or denied.
